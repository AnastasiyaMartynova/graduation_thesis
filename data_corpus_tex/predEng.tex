\noindent
     The jubilee 20th issue of the Annual Proceedings of the Institute of Informatics Problems 
     of the Russian Academy of Sciences (IPI RAN) continues a tradition of presenting the main 
     results of R\&D activities of the scientists of IPI RAN. It consists of three volumes.
     
     The present volume (No.\,1) contains original articles 
     on the most notable IPI RAN's achievements in 2010 in the following research areas: information 
     technologies for accumulation, storage, retrieval, data processing and display, and architectural 
     and system solutions for developing new generation of computation complexes and networks.
     
     Among the authors are the leading scientists of IPI RAN\linebreak  (Yu.~Step\-chen\-kov, 
     Yu.~Diachenko, Yu.~Rozhdestvenski, V.~Za\-kha\-rov, B.~Shmeilin, O.~Arkhipov, Z.~Zykova,
     etc.) as well as young  researchers and PhD students.
     
     The volume includes the complete bibliography of the scientific papers, published by the 
IPI RAN researchers in 2009, and represents the main directions of scientific
studies in IPI RAN and most prominent recent results.

The volume is prepared as a reference for research scientists and practicing engineers in the field 
of informatics and computer science and can be useful for university postgraduates who are interested 
in the current state of research activities in this area.
